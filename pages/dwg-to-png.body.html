How to convert a DWG to an image

  1. Open the converter and drop in your DWG or DXF file.
  2. Tick the sheets or drawings you want.
  3. Under More options, set Save as to PNG image or JPG image. (The buttons above set this for you.)
  4. Click Download image. Several sheets are saved as separate images in one zip file.

PNG or JPG?

  • PNG keeps every line crisp, with no blur around thin lines and text. It is the better choice for drawings.
  • JPG files are smaller and are accepted everywhere, for example by some upload forms and older programs.

Images are about 4000 pixels on the long side, on a white background, which is sharp enough to print at A4 or to zoom in on screen.

Tips for a clean image

  • Hide layers first. Open the large preview and switch off layers you do not want, such as construction lines or notes. They are left out of the image.
  • Black and white or colour. Black and white draws every line in black with its lineweight. Colour keeps the drawing's colours.
  • Need to zoom right in? A PDF stays sharp at any zoom, because it keeps the lines as vectors.
