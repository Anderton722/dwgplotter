How to convert a DXF to PDF

  1. Drop your DXF file into the box at the top of this page, or click it to choose the file. The converter opens with your drawing.
  2. If the model holds several separate drawings, Smart plot lists each one on its own. Tick the drawings you want.
  3. Choose black and white or colour, and the paper size under More options.
  4. Click Download PDF. Several drawings can go into one combined PDF or separate files.

What you get in the PDF

  • Vector lines. The PDF keeps lines as vectors, so it stays sharp when you zoom in or print large.
  • Lineweights and linetypes from the DXF, so dashed and centre lines print as drawn.
  • Black and white prints every line in black, like a monochrome plot style. Colour keeps the drawing's colours.
  • Fitted to the paper. Each drawing is fitted to the sheet size you choose: A4, A3, A2 or A1.

What is supported

DXF is an exchange format that many CAD, CAM and design programs can save. The converter reads the common objects: lines, polylines, arcs, circles, ellipses, splines, text, blocks, dimensions, leaders and filled areas (hatches), along with layers, linetypes and lineweights. You can switch layers off in the preview first, and they are left out of the PDF.

Limits to know about

  • Model drawing only. Sheet layouts are not read from DXF files, so you get the drawing in the model. For layouts, use the original DWG.
  • Some objects are skipped, such as points and block attribute text.
  • Text. It uses a similar single-stroke font, so letter shapes can differ slightly from the CAD program's plot.
  • Check an important drawing against the original before sending it out.

Questions

Is the DXF to PDF converter free?

Yes. No sign-up, no watermark and no limit on files.

Is my drawing uploaded?

No. The file is read in your browser. See the privacy policy.

Can I convert a DXF to PDF without AutoCAD?

Yes. No CAD program is needed, only a web browser on Windows, Mac, Chromebook or Linux.

Can I just look at the DXF first?

Yes. Open it in the DXF viewer to zoom, measure and switch layers, then save the PDF from there.

Can I save a DXF as an image instead?

Yes, as PNG or JPG. See DWG or DXF to PNG.

What is the difference between DWG and DXF?

DWG is AutoCAD's own format. DXF is a text-based exchange format that other programs also read and write. DWG files keep sheet layouts, which is why we recommend DWG when you have it.
