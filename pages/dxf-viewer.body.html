Free online DXF viewer, right in your browser

View DXF files online without installing anything. Zoom in, switch layers on and off, measure, and save it as a PDF or image. The file is read on your computer and never uploaded.

Open a DXF file

How to view a DXF file online

  1. Drop the DXF file into the box at the top of this page, or click it to choose the file. The viewer opens with your drawing.
  2. The drawing appears as a preview. If the model holds several separate drawings, Smart plot shows each one on its own.
  3. Click a preview to open it large, then zoom, pan, hide layers or measure.

DXF files from any program

DXF is the exchange format that most CAD, CAM and design programs can save, from architecture software to laser-cutter, plasma and CNC tools, sign-making software and vector drawing programs. The viewer reads the usual objects: lines, polylines, arcs, circles, ellipses, splines, text, blocks, dimensions, leaders and hatches, along with layers, colours, linetypes and lineweights.

Check a DXF before laser cutting or CNC

  • Size. Use Measure on a known edge to confirm the drawing is in the units you expect, millimetres or inches.
  • Stray parts. Smart plot shows each separate drawing on its own, so a forgotten part or a stray line far from the drawing is easy to spot.
  • Layers. Switch off the engraving or annotation layers to see only the cut lines.
  • Text. Make sure lettering that will be cut is there and in the right place.

Good to know

  • Model drawing only. Sheet layouts are not read from DXF files. If you have the original DWG, open that in the DWG viewer to see the sheets.
  • Text uses a similar single-stroke font, so letter shapes can differ slightly from the CAD program.
  • Points and block attribute text are not drawn.

Questions

Is this DXF viewer free?

Yes. No sign-up, no watermark and no limit on files. It is open-source software.

Is my DXF uploaded?

No. It is read inside your browser. See the privacy policy.

Can I convert the DXF to PDF?

Yes. Tick the drawings you want and click Download PDF. See DXF to PDF for the details.

Does it work on a Mac, Chromebook or phone?

Yes. It runs in Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox on any computer, tablet or phone.

Can I edit a DXF here?

No. You can view, measure, switch layers and save as PDF or an image. To change the drawing you need CAD or CAM software.

What is the difference between DXF and DWG?

DWG is AutoCAD's own compact format. DXF is a text-based exchange format that other programs can read and write. See what is a DWG file.
