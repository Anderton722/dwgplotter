How to open a DWG file on a Mac

macOS has no built-in way to open DWG drawings, and Preview and Quick Look only show a blank icon. The fastest fix needs no download: open the drawing in your browser.

Open a DWG in Safari or Chrome

Option 1: in your browser (no install)

  1. Drag the DWG file from Finder onto the box at the top of this page, or click it to choose the file. It works in Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Edge.
  2. Every sheet is shown as a preview. Click one to zoom, switch layers on and off, or measure.
  3. To keep a copy you can open anywhere, click Download PDF. The PDF opens in Preview.

The drawing is read on your Mac and is not uploaded, so this is safe for client and confidential drawings. It works on Intel and Apple silicon Macs alike, with nothing to download.

Why Preview and Quick Look cannot open a DWG

DWG is a CAD format, not an image or document format. macOS only understands formats it has a reader for, such as PDF, JPG and PNG, and it has none for DWG. That is why Finder shows a plain icon and Preview reports that it cannot open the file. Opening the drawing in a DWG viewer, or turning it into a PDF, solves both.

Option 2: ask for a PDF

If you only need to look at the drawing, the sender can export a PDF in a minute. This gives the exact fonts and plot settings they intended.

Option 3: CAD software for Mac

If you need to edit the drawing, you need CAD software. AutoCAD has a Mac version, and there are other CAD programs for macOS that read DWG. Most are paid, and they are large downloads, so they are worth it only if you work with DWG files regularly.

Open a DWG on an iPhone or iPad

The same page works in Safari on iOS and iPadOS. Tap the box, choose the file from Files, Mail or iCloud Drive, and the sheets appear. An iPad is comfortable for most sheets; on a phone, small sheets read fine and large A1 sheets are easier to check on a bigger screen.

Tips for Mac users

  • DWG in an email or a zip? Save it to Downloads first, and unzip it if needed, then drag it onto the page.
  • Several linked files? Select them all in Finder and drag them on together. See xrefs.
  • Not sure which AutoCAD version saved it? Use the DWG version checker.
  • It will not open? See DWG file won't open.

Questions

Is there a free DWG viewer for Mac?

Yes. This one runs in Safari or Chrome on your Mac, is free, needs no account and nothing to download. See the free online DWG viewer.

Can I open a DWG on a Mac without AutoCAD?

Yes. To view, measure or print a drawing you need no CAD software at all, only a web browser.

Is my drawing uploaded?

No. The file is read on your Mac inside the browser and is not sent to any server.

Can I edit a DWG on a Mac for free?

Not with this tool, which views and converts only. Editing a DWG needs CAD software for macOS.

How do I turn a DWG into a PDF on a Mac?

Drop the DWG in, tick the sheets you want and click Download PDF. See DWG to PDF.
