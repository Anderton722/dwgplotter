How to open a DWG file without AutoCAD

A DWG is AutoCAD's drawing format. If someone sends you one and you do not have the software, you can still open it free, in your browser, in under a minute.

Open a DWG in your browser

Option 1: open it in your browser

  1. Drop the DWG file into the box at the top of this page, or click it to choose the file.
  2. Each sheet (layout) appears as a preview, plus the model drawing.
  3. Click a preview to open it large. Zoom, switch layers on and off, and measure distances.
  4. To keep or print it, tick the sheets and click Download PDF.

The DWG viewer reads the file on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded, so it is a sensible choice for client or confidential drawings, and it works on Windows, Mac, Chromebook and Linux without installing anything.

Option 2: ask for a PDF

The person who sent the drawing can usually export a PDF in a minute. This is the best route when you need the sheet exactly as plotted, because they can use the right fonts and plot settings. If that is not possible, the converter above is the quickest alternative.

Option 3: a free viewer from Autodesk

Autodesk, the maker of AutoCAD, provides free ways to view DWG files, such as a desktop viewer for Windows and an online viewer. They may need an installation or an Autodesk account, and online viewers upload your file, so check their terms if the drawing is confidential.

What you can and cannot do without AutoCAD

  • You can read the drawing, zoom in, check layers, measure, and print or save a PDF.
  • You cannot edit the drawing. Editing needs CAD software.

Tips

  • Missing parts? The drawing may link to other DWG files (xrefs). Ask the sender for them and drop them in together with the main file.
  • Blank page? Some drawings have no layouts and everything sits in the model. The converter's Smart plot finds the real drawings in that case.
  • Old file? Drawings from R14 onwards open. Check yours with the DWG version checker.
  • DXF instead of DWG? Use the DXF viewer.

Questions

How can I open a DWG file for free?

Drop it into the free DWG viewer on this page. It opens in your browser with no sign-up and no install, and the file stays on your computer.

Can I open a DWG in Word, Excel or a PDF reader?

No. Those programs cannot read DWG. Turn the drawing into a PDF first, then it opens anywhere. See DWG to PDF.

Why won't my DWG file open?

The usual causes are an unfinished download, a file still inside a zip, an unusual DWG version or missing linked files. See DWG file won't open.

Can I open a DWG on my phone?

Yes, in the phone's browser. Large sheets are easier to check on a computer.

What is a DWG file?

A technical drawing from AutoCAD or other CAD software, such as a floor plan. See what is a DWG file.
