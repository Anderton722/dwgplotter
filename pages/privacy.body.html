Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

Your drawings

When you open a DWG or DXF file, it is read and converted by code running in your own web browser. The file is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored. Closing the page discards it. The source code of this site is public, so anyone can check this.

What is stored on your device

The site remembers a few choices, such as black and white or colour and your preferred paper size, in your browser's local storage so you do not have to choose again. This stays on your device. We do not set cookies.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not track you across sites. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, which pages are viewed, which countries visitors come from, and which sites send them here. It sets no cookies and does not identify or follow individual visitors. We do not know who you are or what drawings you open.

Hosting

The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and the pages you request, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. The site loads its scripts and fonts from its own address, apart from Cloudflare's analytics script.

Advertising

We may show adverts on this site in future. If we do, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads. Before any adverts appear we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Adverts will never have access to your drawings.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If that changes, we will say so here. You can contact us with any privacy question using the link below.
