Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

Your drawings

When you open a DWG or DXF file, it is read and converted by code running in your own web browser. The file is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored. Closing the page discards it. The source code of this site is public, so anyone can check this.

What is stored on your device

The site remembers a few choices, such as black and white or colour and your preferred paper size, in your browser's local storage so you do not have to choose again. This stays on your device. We do not set cookies ourselves, but our advertising partner may (see Advertising below).

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not track you across sites ourselves (for the advertising partner, see Advertising below). We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, which pages are viewed, which countries visitors come from, and which sites send them here. It sets no cookies and does not identify or follow individual visitors. When you open or save a drawing, the page also sends us just the type of event (for example "PDF saved") so we can count how often the tool is used. That message carries no file name, no file contents and nothing that identifies you. We do not know who you are or what drawings you open.

Hosting

The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and the pages you request, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. The site loads its scripts and fonts from its own address, apart from Cloudflare's analytics script and Google's advertising script.

Advertising

This site shows adverts served by Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where the law requires it, for example in the UK and the European Union, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. Adverts never have access to your drawings: files are read only inside your browser and are not sent anywhere.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If that changes, we will say so here. You can contact us with any privacy question using the link below.
